Privacy Policy

For SimplyNote · Last updated: November 16, 2026

Innovexa Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the SimplyNote mobile application ("App").

1. Information You Add to the App

SimplyNote lets you create and manage notes, including note titles, written content, categories, preferences, and any images you choose to add. This information is used to provide the App's note-taking features.

Storage: The current app implementation is designed to save notes and app preferences on your device using local storage. Information saved only on your device is not automatically available to us. If a future version adds account, cloud-sync, backup, analytics, or other online services, this section must be updated to explain those services and their data handling.

2. How We Use Information

Information you enter is used to operate the App's features, such as:

  • Creating, editing, organizing, and deleting notes.
  • Managing categories, favorites, pinned notes, and Trash.
  • Remembering app preferences and settings.
  • Responding to support requests that you send to us.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We do not access or share note content stored locally on your device unless you choose to share it through a feature or service on your device.

If the App is configured with advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, cloud storage, or other third-party SDKs, those providers may process certain information under their own privacy policies. The developer should list those providers here before publishing this policy if they are included in the released app.

4. Permissions

The App may request access to device features, such as photos or media, when you choose to add an image to a note. Permission access is used for the feature you request. You can review or change app permissions in your device settings.

5. Data Security and Retention

Notes stored locally remain on your device until you delete them or remove the App and its data. Items moved to Trash may remain in the App until you restore them or permanently delete them. You are responsible for keeping your device secure and maintaining any backups you need.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security of information stored on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us so we can review the request.

7. Your Choices

You can edit or delete notes within the App. You can also manage permissions through your device settings. Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored app data, depending on your device and backup settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its practices change. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Please review this page periodically.
